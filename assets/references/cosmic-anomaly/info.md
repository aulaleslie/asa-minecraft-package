# 🌌 Cosmic Anomaly: Full Lore & Profile

### 📋 Basic Profile & Stats
* **Entity Type:** Cosmic Giant / Guardian Creature
* **Estimated Width:** 750,000,000 kilometers (approx. 466 million miles)
* **Estimated Weight:** 800,000,000,000 tons (approx. 800 billion tons)
* **Alignment:** Protective / Non-destructive

### 🎨 Visual Description
* **Form:** Appears as a gargantuan, light-yellow glowing oval ring resembling a giant letter "O".
* **Central Void:** Features a pitch-black hollow center that functions like a massive cosmic eye staring through the dark.
* **Appendages:** Possesses four dull-yellow, ribbon-like space tentacles extending from its main body out into the starry vacuum.

### 📚 Lore & Behavior
* **Universe:** A fan-made community creation residing within the collaborative Trevor Henderson-inspired "Giants" mythos.
* **Role:** Unlike the majority of terrifying and destructive internet horror entities, the Cosmic Anomaly serves as a cosmic protector. 
* **Scale:** Operating on an interstellar level, its sheer scale completely dwarfs entire planets and solar systems, drifting silently through deep space.

### ⚡ Abilities & Powers
* **Plasma Laser Voids:** Can discharge highly concentrated, devastating plasma beams directly from its central void, reaching temperatures of billions of degrees Celsius.
* **Telekinesis:** Capable of manipulating celestial objects and matter across immense cosmic distances.
* **Immeasurable Durability:** Exhibits absolute resilience, making it completely immune to standard physical and cosmic damage.
* **Reality-Shattering Shockwaves:** Possesses the power to generate and project reality-bending concussive shockwaves through the vacuum of space.
