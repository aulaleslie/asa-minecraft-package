# The Interdimensional Wandering Intruder

The Interdimensional Wandering Intruder is an omniversal, god-like space entity that roams through different dimensions and outer space environments. 

---

### Lore

* **Origin:** Documented within fan-made expansions of the *Trevor Henderson Creations* community.
* **Alignment:** Classified as **Neutral**. 
* **Behavior:** 
  * It does not actively hunt human beings or seek the destruction of Earth out of malice.
  * It wanders passively across the cosmos, operating on a scale completely disconnected from human reality.
  * Its physical presence defies conventional structural logic, appearing as a massive silhouette against starfields due to its multi-dimensional nature.

---

### Traits

* **Physical Appearance:**
  * **Form:** Humanoid, shadow-like silhouette with elongated, spindly limbs.
  * **Facial Features:** Features a single, glowing red eye dominating its head structure.
  * **Dimensions:** Vast physical height, with immeasurable width and length that exist outside normal physical geometry.
* **Dietary Preferences:**
  * **Favors:** Consuming entire planets and black holes.
  * **Avoids:** Explicitly dislikes eating whole galaxies.
