# Universe Destroyer

Based on the fanmade lore within the *The giants Trevor Henderson creations Wiki*, here is the breakdown of this entity's traits and lore.

## 🌌 Lore
* **Classification:** Ranked among the "Omniversial" entities and categorized under the "Largest Giants" within its fictional universe.
* **Cosmic Power:** True to its name, it is an exceptionally destructive cosmic force capable of completely obliterating entire universes. 
* **Forms & Scaling:** It possesses multiple stages of power. In its second form, it escalates significantly, making it canonically stronger than other high-tier community giants like *Poyraz.exe*.

## 👁️ Traits
* **Appearance:** It manifests as a massive, elongated purple cosmic form with three distinct crests or spikes at its rear, drifting through deep outer space.
* **Glowing Eyes:** Its most defining features are its two large, uneven, glowing yellow-green eyes that completely lack pupils.
